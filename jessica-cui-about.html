ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Jessica Cui, a concept designer dedicated to crafting compelling character archetypes and immersive worlds. I am constantly pushing myself to evolve creatively and tackle complex visual challenges within the entertainment industry. When I'm not designing, you can usually find me reading up on history, liberal arts, and cultural studies, or sinking hours into RPG, ARPG, and TRPG video games.

Observing real people and exploring the nuances of human characteristics is my true core drive. I find it endlessly intriguing how a person's background, environment, and personal taste dictate their appearance and the outfits they wear. Whether I'm analyzing the distinct fashion silhouettes of the past or simply people-watching today, I love translating those grounded, real-world details into compelling character designs and concept art.
